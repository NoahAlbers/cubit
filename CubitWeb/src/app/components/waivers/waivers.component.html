DocuSeal connection

Documents and signature fields are designed in DocuSeal. Cubit publishes versions, opens individual signing requests, and verifies completion with the DocuSeal API.

The local demo works without an account. To test DocuSeal, configure a sandbox key on the server, restart Cubit, and publish a waiver with a DocuSeal template ID. Keep API keys out of waiver text.
