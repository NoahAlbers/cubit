Sign-in & security

An authenticator adds a six-digit code to your password when you sign in. Use a trusted authenticator app and keep your phone's time set automatically.

  1. Confirm your current password.
  2. Add the setup key to your authenticator, then enter its current code.
  3. Save the single-use recovery codes privately before signing in again.

Enabling your authenticator ends existing sessions. Recovery codes are shown once and let you sign in if your authenticator is unavailable. Password resets do not remove MFA.

Save or cancel any access-alert edits before starting setup.
