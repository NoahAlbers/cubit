Sign-in & security

An authenticator adds a six-digit code to your password when you sign in. Use a trusted authenticator app and keep your phone's time set automatically.

  1. Confirm your current password.
  2. Scan the QR code or enter the setup key in your authenticator, then enter its current code.
  3. Save the single-use recovery codes privately before signing in again.

Enabling your authenticator ends existing sessions. Recovery codes are shown once and let you sign in if your authenticator is unavailable. Password resets do not remove MFA.

Email alerts

These preferences belong to your account. They will apply only if an operator explicitly enables email delivery in a future live deployment.

Unknown fobs are not assigned to a member. Refused fobs belong to a member but were denied entry. Successful entries never generate email.

The repeat window suppresses additional access alerts for the same fob and outcome. Different fobs or outcomes are handled separately. The default is 15 minutes.

Other categories prepare preferences for future event delivery. Some need a connected payment provider, controller or monitoring service. Once delivery is connected, quiet hours will postpone optional alerts using your selected time zone. There are no recurring overdue reminders or weekly digests.

The preview uses fictional events and sends nothing. Future delivery must recheck your current role; staff-account security alerts require Administration.
